A bijou kitchen in Tankerton

Land + Sea is a small, cosy restaurant in Tankerton, a short walk from the crowds of Whitstable town centre.

Two chefs cook everything in the open-plan kitchen, so you catch the aromas as you walk in. The menu is built for sharing and can change daily.

  • Menu changes daily
  • Open kitchen
  • Weekend brunch
  • Kent wines
