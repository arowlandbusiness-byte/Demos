Breakfast by the beach

Beach Cafe sits on Beach Walk in Whitstable, serving breakfasts freshly cooked to order, from a full English to vegan plates.

It's a favourite after the Saturday parkrun or a walk along the seafront, and takeaway can be ordered by phone.
