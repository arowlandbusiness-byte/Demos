A village pub in Whitstable

The Monument is a comfortable, village-style pub on the edge of Whitstable, in a Grade II listed 18th-century building opposite the historic All Saints Church.

It's made for families and friends alike: food is served every day, the garden is large with a play area, and dogs are welcome.
