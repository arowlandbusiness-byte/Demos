Wine, records and Ramsgate

Penelope was founded by Jody and Jim Breese, who brought their experience of the international bar and DJ scenes in Ibiza and Bali back to their hometown of Ramsgate.

It's a natural wine bar, a bottle shop and a vinyl-listening room in one, with a high-quality sound system and regular DJ sets at the weekend.
