Turkish cooking in the village

The Minster Meze & Grill is a traditional Turkish restaurant and takeaway on Monkton Road. The grill is open to the room, so you can watch your meal being cooked.

Eat inside, or out in the enclosed garden, where the tables have their own shelters.
