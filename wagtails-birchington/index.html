A small, friendly café

Wagtails is a small café and bar on Minnis Road, run by Lorrain with the help of her team, Jodie and Stacey. You might remember it as Joes Cafe & Bar.

It's part of the Chatty Cafe Scheme, so there's always a space at the Chat and Natter table for anyone who fancies a bit of company.
