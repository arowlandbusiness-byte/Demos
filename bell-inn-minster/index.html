Five centuries on the High Street

The Bell Inn is a Grade II listed public house dating from the 16th century, reclad and extended in the mid-1800s. It sits right at the heart of Minster village.

Today it's a large, family-friendly pub with real ales, wine from a local supplier and a big, varied menu, all homemade and cooked to order.
