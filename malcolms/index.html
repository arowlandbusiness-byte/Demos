Fifty years in the chair

Malcolm Cox opened the shop on 1 August 1972, three weeks before his eighteenth birthday. He started as an apprentice and ended up owning the building.

Three generations of local families have had their hair cut here. Many of today's barbers began as apprentices in the shop, so the craft has been passed down in the same chairs.
