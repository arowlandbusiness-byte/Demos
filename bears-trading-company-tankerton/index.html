A cosy stop in Tankerton

The Bears Trading Company is a café on Tankerton Road, serving generous lunches, coffee and cake, with ice cream and gifts too.

It's cosy inside whatever the weather, with an outdoor area for sunny days. There's free parking in Tankerton, and the slopes and beach are a short walk away.
