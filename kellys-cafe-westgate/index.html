Westgate's cosy kitchen

Kelly's is a small, quirkily decorated café just off the centre of Westgate-on-Sea, open for breakfast and lunch six days a week.

Portions are generous, there's outdoor seating when the weather allows, and plenty of on-street parking nearby.
