Named after a Broadstairs story

The Magnet opened in February 2019 in the former Reef restaurant on Albion Street. It takes its name from The Magnet, the boys' story paper that featured Billy Bunter, whose creator Charles Hamilton had strong Broadstairs connections.

It's a corner site with big picture windows on two sides, made for watching the town go by over a pint.
