A Broadstairs chippy

Star of the Sea sits on the High Street, close enough to the seafront that most orders end up eaten on the beach wall.

Fish is cooked fresh to order, and chips come in three portion sizes, so you can go light or go all in.

  • Fish cooked fresh
  • Three chip sizes
  • Gluten-free option
  • Takeaway

On the menu

  • Cod, haddock & hakeFried in a light, crisp batter
  • ChipsThree portion sizes, from a lite bite upwards
  • Cod bitesGood for sharing or for smaller appetites
  • Mushy peasThe essential side
  • Gluten-free fish & chipsAsk in store. Cooked in the same oil as the regular fish, so not suitable for coeliacs.
