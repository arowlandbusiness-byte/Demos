A coffee house by the Cathedral

Burgate Coffee House is an independent coffee house close to Canterbury Cathedral, using locally roasted fair-trade coffee.

There's seating on two floors, a counter of homemade cakes (some vegan and gluten-free) and a relaxed atmosphere that's good for reading, working or catching up.

  • Locally roasted coffee
  • Vegan & gluten-free cakes
  • Loose-leaf tea
