A new chapter on The Square

Fields Café is family run, in a building on The Square that was a public house for many years before becoming a café.

The owners named it Fields to mark a new chapter for their family and for the building. The aim is a café that's warm and inviting for everyone, with homemade dishes made from local produce wherever possible.
